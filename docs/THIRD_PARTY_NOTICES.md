# Third-Party Data Notices

## GSHHG-derived ECA geometry

`src/data/eca-zones-web.json` contains coast-clipped polygon coordinates
derived from the Global Self-consistent, Hierarchical, High-resolution
Geography Database (GSHHG), accessed through `basemap-data` 2.0.0. The derived
geometry is distributed under LGPL-3.0-or-later.

- Dataset: Global Self-consistent, Hierarchical, High-resolution Geography Database
- GSHHG version distributed by Basemap: 2.3.6
- Project: https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/mgg/shorelines/shorelines.html
- Citation: Wessel, P. and W. H. F. Smith (1996), *A global, self-consistent,
  hierarchical, high-resolution shoreline database*, Journal of Geophysical
  Research, 101(B4), 8741-8743, doi:10.1029/96JB00104.

The geometry is for map display and preliminary regulatory screening. It is
not an ECDIS product, is not for navigation, and does not replace MARPOL,
official charts, flag/coastal-state guidance, or professional compliance
review.
