# Verdaxis Market Glossary

## Marketplace
The actionable market workspace where a user filters the market by product, port, and availability window, then reviews executable market interest.

## Listing
An actionable row representing a resting bid or ask visible in Marketplace.

## Listings
The Marketplace view that presents actionable filtered rows for the selected market slice.

## Orderbook
The Marketplace view that presents two-sided bid and ask depth for the selected market slice.

## Orderbook Level
A bid or ask depth row in the Orderbook. Selecting a level should take the user to the corresponding Listing for inspection.

## Trade Tape
The public evidence layer for recent completed market activity in the selected market slice.

## My Listings
The Marketplace view that presents the user's own outstanding resting bids and asks.

## Outstanding Listing
A resting bid or ask that is still open or partially filled.

## Trade History
The record of completed or previously initiated trading activity.

## Trade Initiation
The marketplace action that starts a trade confirmation and exposes the activity in Trade History.

## Market Terminal
Archived 2026-07; see ARCHITECTURE.md "Monitoring vs trading surfaces".

## Forward Curve
The market monitoring workspace for scanning approved products and delivery points, reading indicative curve context, and opening a selected market slice in Marketplace when action is needed.

## Market Slice
The canonical market context selected by product, delivery point, and availability window.

## Watched Market
A saved market slice that the user wants to monitor.

## Pinned Listing
A specific resting bid or ask that the user has saved from Listings.

## Market Product
One of the public Verdaxis green fuel products that can be selected in Marketplace.

## Public Marketplace Products
The Marketplace-visible product set: Bio Methanol, e-Methanol, Bio Ethanol, and e-Ethanol.
