## The design-partner offer (terms skeleton)

- **Free seat for 12 months** (supplier listing/demand-signal seat or buyer monitoring seat — the D3 paid SKUs, gratis for design partners).
- **They give:** ≥1 verified indicative bid/ask per week per active slice (non-binding, NDA-covered, redacted on all public surfaces — source names never appear; the redaction is enforced in code and testable), one feedback call per month, permission to count them anonymously ("a licensed Singapore supplier") in the Green Bunker Monitor's contributor tally.
- **They get:** early access to the monthly Green Bunker Monitor, first look at the compliance-denominated pricing overlay (H1) with their own fleet/book, input into the RFNBO qualifier and term-strip design, and founding-contributor status in the eventual index methodology (IOSCO-style contributor recognition without publication of identity).
- **NDA:** mutual; covers indication data, platform roadmap, and their identity as a contributor. Verdaxis's obligations: no identity disclosure, no data resale during pilot, deletion on exit.
